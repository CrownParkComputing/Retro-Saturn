# Retro-Saturn privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026. Retro-Saturn is provided by Crown Park Computing Ltd.

Retro-Saturn does not collect or transmit personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account registration or online artwork service.

The app reads the BIOS, disc images and artwork you select. Your settings, controller layouts, Saturn battery memory and save states remain on your device or in the folder you choose. Folder permissions let the app read your library and write saves; they do not give us access to those files. If you choose a folder managed by a cloud file provider, that provider handles synchronisation under its own privacy policy.

The setup wizard includes a homebrew demo. You supply the Saturn BIOS and any other disc images yourself.

You can revoke folder access in your device settings and remove local app data by uninstalling the app. Files saved in a separately selected folder remain there until you delete them.

App stores may process purchase, installation and diagnostic information according to their own policies. Retro-Saturn does not receive or upload that information through the app.
